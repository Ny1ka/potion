# Potion beta for Mac

Thanks for trying Potion and Potion Dropper before they're released. This page explains how to install them,
what to try, and how to tell us what you find.

- **Potion** converts files on your Mac: video, audio, images, documents and PDFs.
- **Potion Dropper** downloads a video or audio file from a link you're allowed to download from, then converts it.

Both need macOS 13 or later and work on Apple silicon and Intel Macs. Nothing you convert or download is uploaded.

## Install

1. Open `Potion-<version>.dmg` (or `Potion-Dropper-<version>.dmg`) and drag the app onto **Applications**.
2. Open the app from Applications.

This beta isn't signed by Apple yet, so the first time you open it, macOS asks you to confirm:

- **macOS 15 Sequoia or later:** macOS says it can't verify the app. Click **Done**, open **System Settings ›
  Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the message about the app and click **Open Anyway**, then confirm with
  your password. After that it opens normally.
- **macOS 13 or 14:** in Applications, Control-click the app, choose **Open**, then click **Open** in the dialog.

## What to try

**Potion**

- Drop a few files of different kinds on the window (a video, a song, a photo, a Word document, a PDF), pick a
  format for each, and click **Convert All**.
- Double-click a file in the list to see its options (quality, size, pages).
- Drop a whole folder.
- In Finder, select some files, Control-click and choose **Services › Convert with Potion**.

**Potion Dropper**

- Paste a direct link to an MP4 or MP3 you're allowed to download, click **Fetch**, choose **Video** or **Audio**
  and a format, then click **Download**.
- Paste several links at once (one per line) and use **Download All**.
- Stop a download halfway, then click **Retry**: it should continue where it stopped.
- Open **Settings** (⌘,) and try a different save folder or file-name pattern.

## Tell us what you find

Write to **nyikabusiness@gmail.com** with:

- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
- the app and version (in **About**), and your macOS version (Apple menu › About This Mac);
- a screenshot if something looks wrong.

Please don't send copyrighted files you don't have the right to share. A description of the file (its format, size
and where it came from) is usually enough.

## Good to know

- This is a beta: keep your original files until you've checked the converted ones.
- Potion Dropper only downloads from links that point straight at an audio or video file. It says so clearly when
  a site isn't supported.
- **Terms of Use**, **Privacy Policy** and **Acknowledgements** are in each app's **About** window.
