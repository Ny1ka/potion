A small update to **Potion for Mac** with a new logo.

## Download

**[Potion-0.9.2.dmg](https://github.com/Ny1ka/potion/releases/download/v0.9.2/Potion-0.9.2.dmg)**: macOS 13 or later,
Apple silicon and Intel.

- **New to Potion:** open the DMG and drag **Potion** onto **Applications**.
- **Updating from 0.9.0 or 0.9.1:** quit Potion, open the DMG, drag **Potion** onto **Applications** and choose
  **Replace**. Your settings are kept.

This version isn't signed by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time you open it, including after an
update:

- **macOS 15 Sequoia or later:** click **Done**, open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click
  **Open Anyway**, then confirm.
- **macOS 13 or 14:** in Applications, Control-click Potion, choose **Open**, then click **Open** in the dialog.

## What's new

- **A new logo.** Potion's mark is now a lab flask with a straight neck and a cone, filled with the same four colored
  bands for images, audio, video and documents. It replaces the round-bottom flask, which could be mistaken for
  something else. You'll see it in the app icon, the welcome screen, the empty window and About Potion.

macOS keeps app icons in a cache, so Finder or the Dock may show the old icon for a while after you update. Restarting
your Mac clears it.

## Files

- `Potion-0.9.2.dmg`: the app.
- `ffmpeg-sources-0.9.2.tar`: Potion runs FFmpeg (LGPL 2.1 or later) as separate programs. This archive has the
  exact FFmpeg sources and build scripts used, so you can rebuild and replace them.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: checksums. Check a download with `shasum -a 256 Potion-0.9.2.dmg`.
