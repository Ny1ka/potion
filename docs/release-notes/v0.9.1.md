A small update to **Potion for Mac** with a fix in Settings.

## Download

**[Potion-0.9.1.dmg](https://github.com/Ny1ka/potion/releases/download/v0.9.1/Potion-0.9.1.dmg)**: macOS 13 or later,
Apple silicon and Intel.

- **New to Potion:** open the DMG and drag **Potion** onto **Applications**.
- **Updating from 0.9.0:** quit Potion, open the DMG, drag **Potion** onto **Applications** and choose **Replace**.
  Your settings are kept.

This version isn't signed by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time you open it, including after an
update:

- **macOS 15 Sequoia or later:** click **Done**, open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click
  **Open Anyway**, then confirm.
- **macOS 13 or 14:** in Applications, Control-click Potion, choose **Open**, then click **Open** in the dialog.

## What's fixed

- **Settings › Output › Default format for new files** no longer shows an empty choice when the format Potion
  remembered is a different kind of file (for example, video files that you last converted to MP3). The list now
  includes every format a file type can become, with formats of the same kind first.
- Settings opens on the tab you used last.

## Files

- `Potion-0.9.1.dmg`: the app.
- `ffmpeg-sources-0.9.1.tar`: Potion runs FFmpeg (LGPL 2.1 or later) as separate programs. This archive has the
  exact FFmpeg sources and build scripts used, so you can rebuild and replace them.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: checksums. Check a download with `shasum -a 256 Potion-0.9.1.dmg`.
