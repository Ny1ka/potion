The first public version of **Potion for Mac**: convert video, audio, images, documents and PDFs on your own Mac.
Nothing is uploaded.

## Download

**[Potion-0.9.0.dmg](https://github.com/Ny1ka/potion/releases/download/v0.9.0/Potion-0.9.0.dmg)**: macOS 13 or later,
Apple silicon and Intel.

1. Open the DMG and drag **Potion** onto **Applications**.
2. Open Potion from Applications.

This version isn't signed by Apple yet, so the first time you open it, macOS asks you to confirm:

- **macOS 15 Sequoia or later:** macOS says it can't verify Potion. Click **Done**, open **System Settings › Privacy
  & Security**, scroll down to the message about Potion and click **Open Anyway**, then confirm. After that it opens
  normally.
- **macOS 13 or 14:** in Applications, Control-click Potion, choose **Open**, then click **Open** in the dialog.

## What it does

- Drop files or whole folders on the window, pick a format for each file (or one for all), and click **Convert All**.
- **Video:** MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, AVI, M4V, MPEG, WMV, 3GP, TS and more, with quality, resolution, frame rate, codec
  and size options. Extract the audio, or make a GIF.
- **Audio:** MP3, WAV, M4A, ALAC, AAC, FLAC, OGG, OPUS, AIFF and more, with bitrate and sample rate options.
- **Images:** PNG, JPG, WEBP, HEIC, TIFF, BMP, GIF, ICO and AVIF, from those formats and from SVG and camera RAW
  files. Resize, rotate and flip, or combine images into a PDF.
- **Documents:** DOCX, DOC, ODT, RTF, TXT, HTML, Markdown and EPUB, to each other and to PDF.
- **PDF:** turn PDFs into images or documents, and merge, split, extract, reorder or compress them.
- Only conversions that work are offered, and lossy ones say what they'll change before you convert.
- Converted files never replace anything: they're saved next to the originals (or in a folder you choose) with a
  new name. You can also move the originals to the Trash after a successful conversion.
- In Finder, select files, Control-click and choose **Services › Convert with Potion**.

## Privacy

Potion makes no network connections and collects nothing: no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting. See the
[Privacy Policy](https://github.com/Ny1ka/potion/blob/main/legal/PRIVACY.md) and the
[Terms of Use](https://github.com/Ny1ka/potion/blob/main/legal/TERMS.md) (also in Potion's About window).

## Good to know

- This is an early version (0.9.0). Keep your originals until you've checked the converted files.
- Potion doesn't remove copy protection: protected files are refused.
- A Windows version is on the way, after testing on Windows PCs.
- Found a problem? Write to nyikabusiness@gmail.com with what you did, what happened, and your macOS version.

## Files

- `Potion-0.9.0.dmg`: the app.
- `ffmpeg-sources-0.9.0.tar`: Potion runs FFmpeg (LGPL 2.1 or later) as separate programs. This archive has the
  exact FFmpeg sources and build scripts used, so you can rebuild and replace them.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: checksums. Check a download with `shasum -a 256 Potion-0.9.0.dmg`.
